Notifications

Quick message alerts

Turn this on to get notified when a client sends you a quick message about an appointment, and to send them one yourself. No phone number needed.

On an iPhone, notifications only work once this is added to your Home Screen. Safari can't turn them on from inside a regular browser tab.

1. Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up) in Safari's toolbar.
2. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen."
3. Tap "Add" in the top right.
4. Open All Hair Connect from that new icon from now on, then come back here to turn notifications on.

 

Not seeing the message text in a notification? On iPhone, check Settings → Notifications → All Hair Connect → Show Previews, and set it to Always.

Turn this off if you'd rather not be pinged for these, like after hours — your other notifications (quick messages) aren't affected.
